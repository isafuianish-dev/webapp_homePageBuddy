# Home Page Buddy

Your personal command center for every new browser tab — search, bookmarks, a focus list, a weather glance, and a bit of ambience, all on one calm screen.

Home Page Buddy is designed to replace the default new-tab page. Instead of a blank page or a search box with nothing else on it, you get quick access to the sites you visit most, a place to jot down what you're focused on today, the current time and weather at a glance, and a couple of small tools (a stopwatch, a timer, and ambient background music) tucked into the corner for when you need them.

## Features

### Search bar
The centerpiece of the page is a large search box.

- Type anything and press Enter (or click the search button) to search the web.
- The icon on the left side of the search bar shows which search engine you're using. Click it to cycle through **Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, and YouTube** — each click flips to the next engine with a little animation.
- While the search box is empty, its placeholder text quietly types out a rotating inspirational quote. Hover over the small area under the search box to reveal a refresh icon and pick a new quote at random.
- Click into the empty search box and a dropdown of your **recent searches** appears. You can:
  - Click a past search to run it again.
  - Use the **Up/Down arrow keys** to move through your history without touching the mouse (Enter runs the highlighted one, and your original typed text is restored if you arrow back past the top).
  - Hover a history entry and click the small "x" to remove just that one search.
  - Press **Escape**, or click anywhere outside the search bar, to close the dropdown.

### Clock & weather
In the top-left corner, a small pill shows the current time (12-hour clock, updating automatically) next to your local weather.

- The first time you use the page, weather will ask permission to use your location, or you can set one manually.
- Click the weather pill at any time to open **"Set Weather Location."** From there you can:
  - Type a city name to search for it and pick from the matching results.
  - Click **"Use my location"** to let your browser detect where you are.
- Once a location is set, the pill shows a weather icon (sun, clouds, rain, snow, thunderstorm, etc.) and the current temperature in Celsius, and refreshes automatically. Hovering over the pill shows the full location name and conditions.

### Favorites (bookmarks)
Next to the clock/weather pill sits your favorites row — quick-launch tiles for the sites you use most.

- Click the **"+"** tile to add a new favorite: give it a name and a URL (you don't need to type "https://" — it's added automatically if you leave it out).
- Hover over any favorite tile to reveal small **edit** and **delete** buttons above it.
- Click the arrow button at the end of the row to **collapse or expand** the whole favorites row, in case you'd rather have a cleaner screen.

### Focus tasks
In the bottom-right corner is a lightweight to-do list for the day.

- Type into "What are you focused on today?" and press Enter (or click the "+" button) to add a task.
- Check a task off and it moves to the bottom of the list with a strikethrough — and a burst of **confetti** celebrates the win.
- Hover over a task to reveal an "x" button to delete it.
- Use the collapse arrow next to the input to hide or show the task list.

### Utilities bar (stopwatch, timer, music)
In the bottom-left corner are three small tools that expand when you hover over them:

- **Stopwatch** — hover to reveal a play button that starts counting up in minutes:seconds. While running, the elapsed time stays visible even when your mouse moves away. Hover again and click the stop button to reset it to zero.
- **Timer** — hover to see quick presets (5, 10, or 30 minutes) or type a custom time in MM:SS format. Press play to start the countdown; a short beep sound plays when time runs out. The remaining time is visible at a glance even while collapsed.
- **Focus music** — an ambient background music player with four tracks: **Beat, Forest, Relaxation, and Sunset**. Hover to reveal play/pause and "next track" controls. While music is playing, soft animated light streaks radiate gently from the center of the screen in your accent color, giving the page a subtle sense of motion — they fade away automatically when the music is paused.

### Apps Library and Settings
Two small buttons sit in the top-right corner:

- The **grid icon** opens the Apps Library, a launcher listing all of the other "Buddy" apps in this collection.
- The **gear icon** opens **Settings**, where you can adjust the page's theme and accent color (see Customization below).

### Birthday surprise
On June 27th each year, opening the page triggers a special animated effect — soft, oversized "Happy Birthday" text drifts, blinks, and gently zooms across the whole background as a one-day celebration. It appears automatically on that date and doesn't require any action from you.

## How to Use

1. Open the page and start typing to search — or click a favorite tile to jump straight to a site.
2. Set your weather location once by clicking the time/weather pill in the top-left; after that it updates on its own every time you open the page.
3. Add a few favorites for the sites you visit constantly, and a task or two for today's focus.
4. Hover over the stopwatch, timer, or music icons in the bottom-left whenever you need them — they tuck themselves away again when you're not using them.
5. Open Settings (gear icon, top-right) any time to switch between light and dark mode or pick an accent color.

## Tips & Shortcuts

- **Arrow Up / Arrow Down** in the search box cycles through your recent searches; **Escape** closes the dropdown.
- Hovering is the key interaction for the bottom-corner tools (stopwatch, timer, music) and for favorite tiles (edit/delete) and the quote line (refresh) — most controls only reveal themselves when you mouse over them, keeping the page uncluttered until you need something.
- You don't need to type "http://" or "https://" when adding a favorite's URL — it's filled in for you.
- Timers accept either a quick preset (5/10/30 minutes) or a custom time typed as MM:SS (e.g. "12:30").
- If you'd rather not be asked for your location, just search for a city by name instead of using "Use my location."
- Collapse the favorites row or the task list independently if you want a cleaner, more minimal-looking home page — your choice is remembered the next time you open it.

## Customization

Open **Settings** (the gear icon, top-right) to personalize the look of the page:

- **Theme** — switch between light and dark mode.
- **Accent color** — choose from several accent colors (Indigo, Blue, Green, Rose, Amber, Purple) that tint buttons, highlights, and the music visualization.

## Your Data & Privacy

Everything you add — favorites, tasks, your chosen search engine, search history, weather location, and your timer/music preferences — is saved directly in your browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded to any account or server, and none of it is shared between devices or browsers.

A couple of features do talk to outside services in order to work:
- Searching sends your query to whichever search engine you've selected (Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing, or YouTube).
- Setting a weather location sends the location you search for (or your device's coordinates, if you choose "Use my location") to a weather lookup service so it can show current conditions.

If you ever clear your browser's site data, these saved preferences will be reset back to their defaults.
